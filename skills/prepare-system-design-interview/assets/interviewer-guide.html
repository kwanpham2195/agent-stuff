Draw a coherent design

Where does the operation become durable, and who owns that decision?

Optional probe

Illustrative reasoning

The service validates the request, records the result, then replies. Another design may be defensible with an explicit contract.

Explore failures and tradeoffs

What does the caller observe if the reply is lost?

Trace retry, resource limits and recovery against the agreed guarantees.

Optional probe
Which operational signal detects the failure, and how does recovery stop?
Illustrative reasoning

Discuss how a repeated operation is recognized and when that recognition expires. Record unresolved assumptions, not a stack preference.
