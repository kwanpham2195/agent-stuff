Failure scenarios

These prompts illustrate the layout, not mandatory failures for every interview.

The reply is lost

An operation finishes but its caller does not receive the reply.

  • What does the caller observe?
  • What happens if it retries?
  • Which guarantees and limits does your design provide?
A dependency slows down

A dependency responds more slowly while requests continue.

  • Where does work accumulate?
  • How is resource use bounded?
  • How do you detect and recover from the problem?
